# 棋盘可读性

Owner feedback 2026-10-07 (M3 city): putting extra buildings inside the 8x8 board cells' courtyards looked messy/too dense and "不像格子". Keep courtyards empty and each cell a clean "ring" street block on the ±81 m street lines; library models go INTO the ring (replace perimeter modules), not into the courtyard. Also: chimneys must rise from multi-storey factory buildings (as in the reference image), never stand alone on the ground.

**Why:** gameplay readability of the board grid matters more than density; realism means believable building types.

**How to apply:** any future board/city dressing: preserve grid readability first, check against the reference image, show the owner a board-camera screenshot before scaling up.
